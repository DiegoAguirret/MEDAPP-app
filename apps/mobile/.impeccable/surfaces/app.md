# Surface brief — app (all tabs)

Mode: operate

## Direction contract
THESIS: The patient's weight band colors the app, so whose doses are on screen is never in doubt. Refuses the grey list and the generic blue header.
OWN-WORLD: Resuscitation-tape band fills (grey→green) on the patient header and strip. White cards on a cool grey ground, ink #1B2430, brand blue #1F5FAD. Roboto 900 dose figures, category icons on tinted chips.
STORY: The doctor enters the weight once, sees the band, finds the drug in two taps, and reads the volume at arm's length. In an emergency, Urgencias lists every rescue dose for that band.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A band header across the top (band name and range, kg and age, «Nuevo paciente»), then the tape strip, the search, the 2-column category grid with icons, and the A–Z list. Navigation bar at the bottom.
FORM: Weight-band tape, position 1 on the ordered list, chosen as the pick. seed:c06aed157aacb98b
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
